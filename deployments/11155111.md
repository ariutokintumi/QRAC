### Note on deployment cost

Contract creation costs much more on Sepolia today than on our local Cancun-rules chain: 3.98M gas for the verifier (580k locally), and an estimated **10.6M gas** for a full `QRACAccount` (1.54M locally). This supports Phase 2's minimal-proxy factory, where each user account is a tiny clone of one shared implementation.
