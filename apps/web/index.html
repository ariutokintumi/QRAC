Post-quantum seed (24 words)

The seed is independent from any Ethereum wallet: 256 bits of fresh randomness, encoded as 24 BIP-39 words. It controls your QRAC accounts. Your gas-paying wallet does not.

Write these words down, in order. Anyone who has them controls your QRAC accounts.

Importing words alone does not enable signing for accounts that already have activity: also import your latest journal (Manage tab) so one-time keys are never reused.

Cancel the pending action

Signs a CANCEL for the current nonce. Once it lands on-chain, any other signed action for that nonce can never execute. Deleting a local draft is not a cancellation.

Rotate to a new key tree

Builds the next tree (epoch + 1) from your seed and authorizes it with the current key. Old-epoch authorizations stop working. The nonce is never reset.
